Privacy Policy

We keep it simple. TempoTrail does not collect, store or share your personal data. Your progress, coins and settings stay on your device.

Introduction

TempoTrail ("the App") is developed and published by Driftline Games. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App on iOS.

Data We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personal data from users of this App. There is no account, no sign-in and no server that receives your gameplay.

The App uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising data and diagnostic data in order to deliver and measure those ads. You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Local Storage

Your level progress, coins, freezes, ghosts, skips and settings are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to Driftline Games.

Advertising

The App shows rewarded video ads only, and only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a free hint or skip. There are no banner ads and no interstitials.

The App does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking, so the ads you see are not personalised based on your activity in other apps or websites. You can review your tracking settings at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, item bundles and a one-time Coin Doubler). All purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment or financial information.

Children

The App is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
