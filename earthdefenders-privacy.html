Privacy Policy

App:
Earth Defenders: Planet Core
Platforms:
iOS and Android

Last updated: October 16, 2026

We keep it simple. Earth Defenders does not ask for an account, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your progress stays on your device and in your own Apple or Google backup.

Introduction

Earth Defenders: Planet Core ("the App") is developed and published by Driftline Games, Senja, Norway. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App on iOS and Android.

Data We Collect

Driftline Games does not run any server for the App and does not receive your gameplay, your progress or any personal data. There is no sign-up and no chat.

Advertising

The App shows optional rewarded video ads and, now and then, a short ad between battles. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit ads (and prevent fraud), AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking), approximate location from your IP address, and diagnostic data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the App's settings. You can also reset or turn off personalised ads in your device settings. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Buying "No Ads" removes the ads between battles.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (gem packs, the Starter Pack, the Monthly Card and No Ads) are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. The App only keeps the order number on your device so each purchase is delivered once.

Game Center, iCloud and Google backup

On iOS you can sign in to Apple Game Center to see leaderboards; your Game Center name and scores are handled by Apple. Your progress is saved as a Game Center saved game in your own iCloud so it comes back on a new device. On Android, your progress file is included in your device's Google backup (Android Auto Backup) so it can be restored after reinstalling. Driftline Games has no access to either.

Local Storage

Your stages, upgrades, cards, coins, gems and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes them from the device.

Notifications

On iOS the App can send local reminders (for example when your patrol is full). They are scheduled on your device and can be turned off in the App's settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset progress" in the App's settings, or uninstall the App. To remove the iCloud or Google backup, delete it in your device's iCloud or Google account settings. Questions or requests: email us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be shown by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at support@driftlinegames.com.
